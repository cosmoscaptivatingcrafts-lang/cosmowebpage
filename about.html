MEET THE FOUNDER

Built From Creativity, Family & Determination

Crafts by Cosmo grew from a love of creating personalized items for family celebrations and special moments. What began with a few tools and a creative idea became a business centered on helping people make important moments memorable.

Today, the business continues to grow through custom gifts, event creations, apparel, décor, memorial pieces and business promotional products.
